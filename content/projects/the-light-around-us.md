SUBTITLE:
A 10,800-LED sculpture that breathes with the air and sound of the room around it

BODY:
In 2019, Dashboard and tvsdesign asked a group of artists to make a "breathing" artwork for SPACELAB, one that responds to the quality of the air and the sounds around us. Dashboard conceived and curated the project, and tvsdesign collaborated on it and sponsored it.

What came out of it is a one-of-a-kind generative sculpture of 10,800 LEDs. Occupancy sensors inside SPACELAB read temperature, humidity, and sound vibration in real time, and the piece turns those readings into light and color. When the room changes, the sculpture changes with it.

The form is a run of curved white ribs that rise from the floor and sweep overhead, each one lined with LEDs. Arc Design fabricated it. Lit, it reads as a single surface of moving color that people stand under and walk through.

We think of it as a prototype for a new kind of sensory communication. Temperature, humidity, and sound are conditions you feel but rarely see. Here they become something you can read across a room.

OUTCOME:
